About V Mind

Our Mission & Approach

V Mind is dedicated to providing compassionate and professional counselling services. We believe in a personalized, evidence-based approach to help our clients navigate life's complexities, heal from past traumas, and develop a resilient mindset for a fulfilling future. Our certified professionals are here to support your journey towards mental wellness.

Meet Our Expert

Ranjini | Founder

With over a decade of experience in individual and couple counselling, Ranjini specializes in trust rebuilding therapy and holistic mental health approaches. Her empathetic and client-centered methods have transformed countless lives.

Ranjini

Personal counsellor

Ranjini is a dedicated and compassionate consultant psychologist with a Master's in Clinical Psychology and a certification as a family therapist. With a specialization in therapeutic modalities such as CBT, DBT, EFT, and FT, she is committed to helping individuals and couples navigate the complexities of their lives. Her expertise includes couple and family counseling, trust and breakup therapy, sex therapy, and bystander counseling. Ranjini's goal is to provide a safe and non-judgmental space where clients can explore their emotions, overcome challenges, and build a more resilient mindset. She also offers professional training and supervision for aspiring counselors.
